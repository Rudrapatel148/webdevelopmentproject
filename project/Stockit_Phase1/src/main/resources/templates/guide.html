User Guide

  1. Open Inventory to browse saved products.
  2. Use Category and Stock Status to filter the list.
  3. Choose a sort field and direction, then select Apply.
  4. Click Add Product, complete all fields and submit.
  5. Correct any validation errors shown under the fields.
  6. After saving, the product detail page confirms persistence.

Low stock: quantity less than or equal to reorder level.
